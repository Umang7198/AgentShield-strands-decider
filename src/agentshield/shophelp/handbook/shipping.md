# Shipping and address changes — Hearth & Cable

We ship from the UK. Tracking ids look like `HC-1001-UK`.

## Status meanings

- **processing** — not yet handed to the carrier. Address changes are allowed without a supervisor. Cancellations are allowed.
- **shipped** — with the carrier. Address changes need Priya. Cancellations need a look-up and customer consent; the gate may still allow Maya to ask.
- **delivered** — cannot change address and cannot cancel.
- **cancelled** — already closed.

## Timing

- Standard UK shipping is 3–5 working days after the order leaves processing.
- We do not promise same-day delivery in chat.
- A processing kettle (example: A2002) has no tracking number yet.

## Address changes

- The new address must come from the customer in this conversation. Do not invent a street.
- Shipped orders: Maya proposes `update_shipping_address`; Priya must approve.
- Delivered orders: explain that the parcel cannot be redirected.

## Cancellations

- Look up the order first.
- Ask before cancelling. "Where is my order?" is not consent to cancel.
- Delivered and already-cancelled orders cannot be cancelled.
